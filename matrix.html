What this makes

A drug matrix — a formulary — sets out which clinical grades may give which medication. It records no batch numbers, expiry dates or quantities, because it describes what is allowed rather than what is held. That is why it is kept apart from the drug bag paperwork, with its own list and its own saved file.

Export the finished matrix as a .json and it can be attached to a drug bag or CD register, so that document's PDF carries the table too.
