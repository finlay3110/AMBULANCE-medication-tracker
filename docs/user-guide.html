1. What it does

Drug Bag Tracker turns a list of what you hold into printable A4 paperwork. It makes two separate documents:

  • A drug bag — a contents label for the outside of the bag, then a numbered usage log for every medication so each dose can be signed out.
  • A controlled drugs register — a front sheet for the safe, a running-balance register page for each drug, a stock check record, and a sign-out sheet for stock taken from the safe into a personal pouch.

Controlled drugs are held in the CD safe, not in a drug bag, so the two are kept completely apart. Each keeps its own details and its own list, and you can prepare both without them mixing.

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is sent anywhere, and it works with no internet connection once the page is open.

2. Getting started

Open index.html in a browser, or visit the address your organisation hosts it at. There is nothing to install and no account to create.

Work runs left to right across three tabs:

  1. Setup — who you are and which bag or safe this is.
  2. Medications — what is in it.
  3. Generate — download the PDF.

Your work is saved as you type, in this browser, on this device. It is not a backup: clearing site data, a private window, or a different computer will not have it. Section 9 covers keeping a copy properly.

3. Setup

Document type

Pick Drug bag or Controlled drugs (CD safe) first — it changes the rest of the form and the document you get at the end. Switching between them does not disturb the other one, so you can prepare both in one sitting. The whole interface turns dark red on the CD side so you can tell at a glance which you are editing.

Start from a saved file

If you exported a bag or register before, load it here and update the batch numbers and expiry dates. That is much the quickest way to prepare a repeat bag. The document type comes from the file, so importing a register while you are on the bag side switches you over.

Company details

Name, contact number and address appear on the label and in the “if found” notice. These are the same on every bag you ever make, so save them once with Export company details and load them into anything new with Import company details. The file carries the logo and CQC registration too, and nothing about any particular bag.

CQC registered is a toggle, because not every organisation is registered. Tick it and a registration number is asked for; that number is then printed beside your company name on the label and register front sheet. Leave it off and nothing about CQC appears anywhere.

Company logo takes a PNG or JPEG, drawn at the top of the label on a white panel so a dark logo still reads. It is scaled down before being stored and travels with your exports.

Bag or safe details

In service until is worth understanding. Left blank, expiry is judged against today. Set it to the date the bag is due back and expiry is judged against that instead — so stock that is in date now but runs out while the bag is away gets flagged, in the app and in a band on the label. It is the difference between “is this in date?” and “will it still be in date when this comes back?”

4. Adding medications

Quick add

Pick an entry and it fills the name, formulation, strength and a suggested legal category, leaving you the batch, expiry and quantity. Everything stays editable afterwards.

Two options are always offered at the bottom of the list:

  • — other strength or formulation fills only the name, for when you hold a different presentation from the one listed.
  • Not on the list — enter it manually clears the form so you can type anything at all. The list is a shortcut, never a restriction.

The form

Expiry

Most packs state a month, so the field is a month picker by default and treats the stock as in date to the last day of that month. Switch it to Exact date for packs that print a full one. The line underneath confirms how it was read before you add anything.

Quantity

The number of doses in the bag — that is how many numbered rows the usage log gets. On a CD register this is the quantity held, and you also give a unit (ampoules, ml, mg…) which is printed in the register's balance columns so a figure can never be read as the wrong measure.

More than one batch of the same drug

Add it twice. Use the Copy button on an entry to prefill a new one with the name, formulation and strength, then type the second batch, expiry and quantity. Each batch gets its own log page — and for a controlled drug its own running balance, which is what you want, since one balance cannot span two batches.

The list

Red flags mean already expired, or expiring before the bag is due back. Amber means within 90 days. Both appear again on the generate tab and on the label itself.

5. Generating the document

Download PDF saves the document; Preview in new tab opens it without saving. If anything required is missing, you are told what before anything is produced.

The PDF cannot be loaded back in. It is the printable record. The .json is the editable one — without it, next month's version of this bag has to be typed from scratch. Save both.

6. What the drug bag PDF contains

The label carries your logo, company name and CQC registration, the bag number, who prepped and checked it, the prepped date, the seal number, and the bag expiry — the earliest expiry it contains, because that is when the bag as a whole stops being usable. The contents table colour-codes each legal category and shows anything expired or expiring in red.

Below it, a two-person check block to sign, and the if found notice asking a finder to call you or hand the bag to a police station.

If anything expires before the in-service date, a red band lists exactly what and when.

Each usage log repeats the medication, presentation, dose, batch and expiry at the top, then gives one numbered row per dose with date used, PRF number and a signature. A medication with more doses than fit on a page is split evenly across pages rather than leaving a stub.

Every page footer carries the bag, the company, when the PDF was generated, and “Page 3 of 9” — so a missing sheet is obvious and you can tell two printouts apart.

7. What the CD register contains

The front sheet lists the safe reference and location, the accountable officer, the stock held with quantities and units, a two-person stock check block, and a storage and handling notice.

Each register page opens with the balance brought forward, then takes date, time, PRF number, amount given, amount discarded, running balance, and separate administering and witness signatures. Spare rows beyond the stock count give discards and part doses a line of their own.

Both are landscape. The stock check record continues onto further sheets past seven drugs rather than squeezing the columns.

The register is a convenience format. It does not constitute, replace or discharge any statutory record-keeping duty. Satisfy yourself that it meets the requirements that apply to you before relying on it, and retain records in line with your own controlled drugs policy and current legislation.

8. Legal categories

CategoryMeaning
GSLGeneral sales list
PPharmacy only
POMPrescription only medicine
S17 Paramedic exemption — Human Medicines Regulations 2012, Schedule 17
S19 Parenteral administration in an emergency — Schedule 19
CD Controlled drug — recorded on the CD register, never in a drug bag

These are suggestions to save typing, not legal determinations.

Medicines can sit in more than one category depending on indication, route and pack size. Legislation is amended over time and the built-in list is a fixed snapshot that does not update itself. The categories were not verified against primary legislation when the list was compiled.

Check every category against current legislation and your own organisation's medicines policy, patient group directions and standard operating procedures before a document is used. Change any of them in the form — nothing is locked.

9. Saving, reusing and backing up

The two file types

FileWhat it is for
Drug-Bag-1-saved.json
CD-Register-2-saved.json		 A whole bag or register — details and every medication. Import it to rebuild or update that document.
Company-Your-Ltd.json Just your organisation: name, contact, address, CQC registration and logo. Load it into anything new.

Importing a whole bag also works as a source of company details, so you can lift them out of any old export.

Preparing next month's bag

  1. Setup → Start from a saved file → import last month's bag.
  2. Update the batch numbers and expiry dates on the medications tab.
  3. Generate, then export the .json again under the new bag number.

Browser storage is not a backup. Clearing site data, a private window, or moving to another computer will lose it, and no responsibility is accepted for work lost that way. Export the .json and keep it somewhere durable — the app tells you when a document has never been exported, or has changed since it was.

10. Dark mode and phones

The interface follows your device's light or dark setting. The Theme button in the top bar cycles auto, light and dark if you want to override it; your choice is remembered. The generated PDF is always light, because it is meant to be printed.

The layout collapses to a single column on a phone, so a bag can be prepped from a handset.

11. Troubleshooting

“Complete the setup tab first”

Something required is empty. The message names each missing field. If you ticked CQC registered, the registration number is required too.

The expiry will not accept what I type

Expiry is picked, not typed. Use the month picker, or switch the dropdown beside it to Exact date. On Safari, which has no month picker, the field accepts typing instead — 07/25, 10/2027 and 18/10/2025 all work.

My medication is not in the quick add list

Use Not on the list — enter it manually and type it. If it is a controlled drug, switch the document type to the CD register on the setup tab.

Preview does not open

Your browser blocked the pop-up, which is common on mobile. Use Download PDF instead.

My work has disappeared

Browser storage was cleared, or you are on a different device, browser, or a private window. Import your .json export. If you have none, it cannot be recovered.

The browser says it will not store my work

Storage is full or blocked. Export the .json immediately — what is on screen is still correct, but it will not survive closing the tab.
