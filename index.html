    Disclaimer, licence and sources

    Disclaimer

    Drug Bag Tracker is a document generator, not a clinical, pharmaceutical or legal reference. Before any document is used, check that every entry matches the stock actually held: name, formulation, strength, batch number, expiry, quantity and legal category.

    The categories offered (GSL, P, POM, S17, S19, CD), including those pre-filled by the quick-add list, are suggestions to save typing, not legal determinations. Medicines can sit in more than one category depending on indication, route and pack size; legislation is amended over time and this list is a fixed snapshot; and the categories were not verified against primary legislation when the list was compiled. Check them against current legislation and your own medicines policy, patient group directions and standard operating procedures.

    The controlled drugs register produced here is a convenience format. It does not constitute, replace or discharge any statutory record-keeping duty. Satisfy yourself that it meets the requirements that apply to you before relying on it.

    Everything is stored in this browser and nothing is uploaded. Browser storage is not a backup — export your documents to JSON and keep those files somewhere durable. No responsibility is accepted for work lost to browser storage being cleared.
